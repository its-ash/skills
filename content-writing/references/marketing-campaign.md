# Marketing Campaign

Plan and execute multi-channel launch campaigns from one product brief: research, positioning, landing page, email sequence, social posts, video scripts, ad copy, and calendar.

## When to use
- You plan a product or feature launch.
- You build a full content suite from one product brief.
- You define positioning and the campaign angle before you write copy.
- You coordinate several content types across channels.
- You review campaign copy for conversion quality and brand consistency.

## Non-negotiables

1. Define positioning before you write copy. All copy comes from the angle.
2. Research the audience before you assume their language or fears.
3. Each deliverable has one clear purpose in the campaign arc.
4. Specificity is better than adjectives on every channel.
5. The same voice runs across all channels and pieces.
6. No copy ships before it passes the quality gate.

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Research

Use [product-strategy/references/market-research.md](../../product-strategy/references/market-research.md) to:
- Profile the target audience (jobs-to-be-done, fears, language, alternatives they use).
- Map 3 or more direct or adjacent competitors (positioning, gaps, messaging weaknesses).
- Identify 1 to 3 audience insights for the campaign angle.

Deliverable: a short research brief (audience profile, competitive summary, key insights).

### Phase 2: Positioning

Produce:
- Core benefit statement (one sentence, no feature list, no jargon)
- Positioning formula: "[Product] helps [audience] [achieve outcome] by [mechanism]"
- Campaign angle: the specific tension, insight, or moment for the whole campaign
- Tone profile: lock it before you write (use [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md))

Do not write copy until the user approves the positioning and angle.

### Phase 3: Content production

Produce in this order. Each layer informs the next.

1. **Landing page copy**: hero, problem, solution, features, how it works, proof, CTA (see [copywriting.md](copywriting.md)).
2. **Email sequence**: one purpose per email. Arc: problem, education, agitation, solution, proof, urgency, final CTA.
3. **Social posts**: platform-native (see [social-content.md](social-content.md)). LinkedIn and X get different formats, not the same copy resized.
4. **Short-form video scripts**: blocked by timestamp, written for screen and ear.
5. **Ad copy variants**: 3 to 4 variants that test different angles or audience segments.
6. **Content calendar**: day-by-day schedule with channel, type, timing, and dependencies.

### Phase 4: Review

- 5-second test on all hero and above-the-fold copy: who it is for, what it does, why act now.
- CTA audit: one per piece, specific, earned.
- Tone consistency across channels.
- Claim audit: each claim is specific and supportable.
- Cross-channel consistency: ad claims match the landing page; the email body matches the subject.

## Output contract

1. **Positioning brief**: angle, core benefit statement, tone profile
2. **Landing page copy**: all sections
3. **Email sequence**: subject, preview, body, and CTA per email, labeled by day and purpose
4. **LinkedIn posts**: 3 or more, each with a different angle
5. **X posts**: 5 or more standalone posts and 1 thread
6. **Short-form video scripts**: 2 or more, blocked by timestamp, with visual direction notes
7. **Ad copy variants**: short headline, long headline, and body per variant
8. **Content calendar**: day-by-day with channel, content type, timing, and dependencies
9. **Copy review summary**: flagged issues and open questions before anything goes live

## Quality gate

- All deliverables sound like the same author.
- No hollow superlatives or filler adjectives remain.
- Each CTA is specific and earned. Never "learn more", "find out more", or "click here".
- No copy is duplicated verbatim across platforms.
- Hero copy passes the 5-second test.
- Email subjects match the email body.
- Ad claims match the landing page claims exactly.
- No copy works unchanged for another product in the category.

Also apply the shared hard bans in [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md). For on-page optimization of the landing page, use [seo](../../seo/SKILL.md).
