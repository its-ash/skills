# Content Writing

Create high‑quality marketing copy that matches a brand’s voice, ranks well in search engines, and fits each publishing platform. The skill also supports collaborative research, outlining, drafting, and iterative feedback for articles, newsletters, case studies, and technical docs.

## When to Use
- You need to define or refine a brand voice.
- You are writing a blog post, article, or newsletter that must be SEO‑friendly.
- You must produce social‑media updates that follow platform best practices.
- You want to plan and schedule content with a calendar.
- You require research, citations, and section‑by‑section feedback while preserving your personal style.

---

## 1. Establishing Brand Voice
### Steps
1. **Collect Existing Content** – Gather any past copy, website text, or marketing assets.
2. **Analyze Tone & Style** – Identify formality, perspective (first‑person vs. third‑person), and recurring adjectives.
3. **Select Voice Attributes** – Choose 3‑5 tone descriptors (e.g., friendly, authoritative, witty) and map them to brand archetypes if you use them.
4. **Document Guidelines** – Create a short brand‑voice guide with:
   - Voice description
   - Preferred vocabulary
   - Prohibited words
   - Example sentences (positive and negative)
5. **Validate Consistency** – Write three sample pieces and compare them against the guide. Refine until the samples match the desired tone.

### Quick Template (Brand‑Voice Guide)
```markdown
# Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Friendly, confident, concise
- **Perspective**: First‑person plural (we)
- **Formality**: Conversational, no jargon unless defined
- **Key Vocabulary**: "you", "our solution", "quick wins"
- **Avoid**: "utilize", overly technical acronyms, passive voice
- **Examples**:
  - ✅ "We help you launch faster."
  - ❌ "Our platform enables utilization of resources."
```

## 2. Writing SEO‑Optimized Blog Posts
### Keyword Research
- Identify a **primary keyword** with 500‑5,000 monthly searches.
- Select 3‑5 **secondary keywords** and 10‑15 **LSI terms**.
- Record search intent (informational, transactional, etc.).

### Content Structure
1. **Title** – Include primary keyword, keep under 60 characters.
2. **Meta Description** – 150‑160 characters, primary keyword, compelling call‑to‑action.
3. **Intro (Hook)** – Place primary keyword within the first 100 words.
4. **Headings** – Use H2 for main sections; embed primary or secondary keywords in at least 2 H2s.
5. **Body** – Aim for 1,500‑2,500 words. Maintain keyword density of 1‑3%.
6. **Internal Links** – Link to 2‑3 related articles on your site.
7. **External Links** – Cite reputable sources (e.g., .gov, .edu, industry leaders).
8. **Images** – Add alt text with relevant keywords.
9. **Conclusion** – Summarize key points and include a clear CTA.

### SEO Checklist
- ✅ Primary keyword in title, URL, first paragraph, and 2‑3 headings.
- ✅ Meta description written and under 160 characters.
- ✅ Keyword density 1‑3% (use a word‑counter tool).
- ✅ Proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3).
- ✅ At least 2 internal and 2 external links.
- ✅ Alt text for every image.
- ✅ Readability score appropriate for audience (e.g., Flesch‑Kincaid 8‑10).

## 3. Social‑Media Content Production
### Platform‑Specific Guidelines
| Platform | Max Length | Ideal Format | Hashtag Tips |
|----------|------------|--------------|--------------|
| Twitter  | 280 chars  | Short hook + link | 1‑3 relevant tags |
| LinkedIn | 1,300 chars| Professional tone, value‑first | 3‑5 industry tags |
| Instagram| 2,200 chars| Visual‑first, emojis OK | 5‑10 niche tags |
| Facebook | 63,206 chars| Conversational, story‑driven | 1‑2 tags |

### Production Steps
1. **Select Core Message** – Pull the main insight from the blog or article.
2. **Adapt Tone** – Adjust formality and length per platform.
3. **Add Visuals** – Use correctly sized images or short videos.
4. **Insert Hashtags** – Use platform‑specific count and relevance.
5. **Schedule** – Use your content calendar (see Section 4) to set optimal posting times.

## 4. Content Calendar Planning
1. **Set Monthly Goals** – Define KPIs (e.g., organic traffic +10%, 5 social shares per post).
2. **Choose Content Pillars** – Typical split: 40 % educational, 25 % promotional, 25 % community, 10 % evergreen.
3. **Map Topics to Dates** – Align with product launches, events, or seasonal trends.
4. **Batch Create** – Write all pieces for the week in one session to maintain voice consistency.
5. **Assign Assets** – Link each piece to required images, videos, or graphics.
6. **Review & Adjust** – At month‑end, compare actual metrics to goals and refine the next month’s plan.

## 5. Collaborative Research & Writing Workflow
### 5.1 Outline Creation
Prompt the assistant:
```
Help me create an outline for an article about [topic].
```
Use the following outline skeleton and fill in sections:
```markdown
# Article Outline: [Title]

## Hook
- Opening line or statistic
- Why the reader should care

## Introduction
- Context
- Problem statement
- What the article will cover

## Main Sections
### Section 1: [Title]
- Key point A
- Evidence / citation needed
### Section 2: [Title]
- Key point B
- Evidence / citation needed
... (repeat as needed)

## Conclusion
- Summary
- Call to action

## Research To‑Do
- [ ] Find data on …
- [ ] Source quote from …
```
Iterate until the outline flows logically and all research gaps are listed.

### 5.2 Research & Citation
When you ask for research, specify the citation style (inline, numbered, footnote). The assistant will:
1. Locate credible sources (industry reports, academic papers, reputable news).
2. Extract key facts, figures, and quotes.
3. Provide citations in the requested format.

**Example Output (Numbered Style)**
```markdown
## Research: Remote Work Productivity

- 73 % of managers report higher team output after adopting hybrid schedules [1].
- A 2024 Gartner study shows a 22 % reduction in meeting time when using AI‑driven agendas [2].

### References
1. Gartner. (2024). *Hybrid Work Impact Survey*.
2. Smith, J. (2024). *AI in Meeting Management*, Tech Review.
```

### 5.3 Hook Improvement
Provide the current hook and ask for alternatives. The assistant returns:
- **Analysis** of what works and what can be stronger.
- **Three revised hooks** with brief rationale.

### 5.4 Section‑by‑Section Feedback
After you finish a section, request feedback:
```
Review the "Why This Matters" section and give suggestions.
```
The assistant replies with:
- Strengths ✅
- Specific improvement suggestions (clarity, flow, evidence, style)
- Line‑by‑line edits where helpful
- Questions to consider for the next revision

### 5.5 Voice Preservation
- Share a few of your existing writings.
- The assistant learns your preferred diction, sentence length, and tone.
- Throughout the process it **suggests** rather than overwrites, asking: “Does this sound like you?”

### 5.6 Final Polishing
When the draft is complete, request a full review. The assistant evaluates:
- Overall structure and flow
- Argument strength and evidence coverage
- Grammar, readability, and citation completeness
- CTA effectiveness
It then provides a concise checklist before publishing.

## 6. Best Practices
- **Start with audience need** – Define the problem you solve before writing.
- **Research first** – Gather keywords, data, and sources prior to drafting.
- **Write the first draft without editing** – Keep momentum.
- **Apply SEO after the draft** – Insert keywords, meta tags, and links.
- **Edit for brand voice** – Use the voice guide to align tone.
- **Proofread** – Check grammar, facts, and citation format.
- **Optimize for each platform** – Adjust length, hashtags, and media.
- **Schedule strategically** – Follow the calendar’s optimal posting times.
- **Measure results** – Track traffic, engagement, and conversion metrics.

## 7. Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Skipping keyword research and then forcing keywords into the copy.
- Ignoring platform character limits or image specifications.
- Over‑optimizing (keyword stuffing, unnatural phrasing).
- Inconsistent voice across pieces.
- Publishing without a final proofread or citation check.
- Forgetting to add a clear call‑to‑action.
- Not reviewing performance data to improve future content.

## 8. Checklist (Before Publishing)
- [ ] Brand‑voice guide consulted and applied.
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, URL, first paragraph, and at least two headings.
- [ ] Meta description written, < 160 chars, includes primary keyword.
- [ ] Keyword density 1‑3% (use a tool to verify).
- [ ] Heading hierarchy correct (H1 → H2 → H3).
- [ ] At least 2 internal and 2 external links added.
- [ ] All images have descriptive alt text with keywords.
- [ ] Readability score matches target audience.
- [ ] Citations formatted consistently and complete.
- [ ] CTA present and compelling.
- [ ] Content scheduled in the calendar with optimal time slot.
- [ ] Final proofread for typos and factual accuracy.

## 9. Performance Metrics to Track
| Metric | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Organic traffic growth | Indicates SEO effectiveness |
| Average time on page | Reflects content relevance and readability |
| Bounce rate | Shows whether expectations match delivery |
| Social shares & likes | Measures audience resonance |
| Backlinks earned | Signals authority and SEO value |
| Conversion rate (lead/form) | Direct business impact |
| Email click‑through rate (if repurposed) | Cross‑channel effectiveness |

Use analytics tools (Google Analytics, social‑media insights) to record these KPIs monthly and adjust your strategy accordingly.
