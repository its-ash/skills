# Executive Advisor

A single reference for CEOs and CTOs. It covers strategic planning, financial modeling, stakeholder management, culture, technology strategy, engineering excellence, board and investor relations, crisis response, and performance metrics.

---

## When to Use
- Defining or revising company vision, mission, and 3‑5‑year strategy.
- Preparing board presentations, investor updates, or fundraising decks.
- Aligning technology roadmaps with business goals.
- Assessing technical debt, scaling engineering teams, or choosing new technologies.
- Managing crises (security breach, major outage, data loss).
- Conducting executive decision reviews or succession planning.

---

## Strategic Planning Cycle
| Quarter | Activities |
|---|---|
| **Q1 – Environmental Scan** | Market analysis, competitive intelligence, technology trends, regulatory review |
| **Q2 – Strategy Development** | Generate strategic options, scenario planning, resource allocation, risk assessment |
| **Q3 – Planning & Budgeting** | Annual operating plan, budget, OKR setting, initiative prioritisation |
| **Q4 – Communication & Launch** | Board approval, investor communication, employee cascade, partner alignment |

### Vision & Mission
- **Vision**: Write a 10‑year aspirational statement.
- **Mission**: Define the purpose and why the company exists.
- **Core Values**: List 3‑5 guiding principles.

## Capital & Resource Management
### Capital Allocation Framework
1. **Core Operations** – 40‑50 %
2. **Growth Investments** – 25‑35 %
3. **Innovation / R&D** – 10‑15 %
4. **Strategic Reserve** – 10‑15 %
5. **Shareholder Returns** – variable

### Fundraising Stages
- **Seed / Series A** – Validate product‑market fit.
- **Series B/C** – Accelerate growth.
- **Late‑Stage / IPO** – Expand market reach.
- **Debt** – Non‑dilutive capital for specific projects.

## Technology Strategy & Roadmap
### Vision & Roadmap
- Define a 3‑5‑year technology vision.
- Break into quarterly roadmaps aligned with business objectives.
- Communicate roadmap to board and product teams.

### Innovation Management
- Allocate **20 %** of engineering capacity for innovation.
- Run quarterly hackathons.
- Evaluate emerging tech via proof‑of‑concepts.

## Technical Debt Management
1. Run a technical‑debt assessment (use your preferred analysis tool).
2. Allocate capacity:
   - **Critical debt** – 40 % of engineering time.
   - **High debt** – 25 %.
   - **Medium debt** – 15 %.
   - **Low debt / maintenance** – remaining time.
3. Prioritise by impact on reliability, security, and delivery speed.

## Crisis Management Playbook
### Incident Response Timeline
- **0‑15 min** – Assess severity, activate incident team, start communication.
- **15‑60 min** – Implement fix, update stakeholders, monitor systems.
- **1‑24 h** – Verify resolution, document timeline, inform customers.
- **48‑72 h** – Conduct post‑mortem, define action items, improve processes.

### Crisis Types & Actions
- **Security Breach** – Isolate systems, involve security team, notify legal/compliance, prepare customer communication.
- **Major Outage** – All‑hands response, update status page, brief executives, reach out to affected customers.
- **Data Loss** – Stop writes, assess recovery options, begin restoration, analyse impact.

## Board & Investor Relations
### Board Package Timeline
- **T‑4 weeks** – Draft agenda.
- **T‑2 weeks** – Prepare materials.
- **T‑1 week** – Distribute package.
- **Day 0** – Conduct meeting.

### Package Components
- CEO Letter (1‑2 pages)
- One‑page KPI dashboard
- Financial review (≤ 5 pages)
- Strategic updates (≤ 10 pages)
- Risk register (≤ 2 pages)
- Appendices as needed

### Investor Pitch Deck Structure
1. Problem (1 slide)
2. Solution (1‑2 slides)
3. Market (1‑2 slides)
4. Product (2‑3 slides)
5. Business Model (1 slide)
6. Go‑to‑Market (1‑2 slides)
7. Competition (1 slide)
8. Team (1 slide)
9. Financials (2 slides)
10. Ask (1 slide)

## Succession & Personal Development
### Succession Timeline
- **Ongoing** – Identify internal candidates, develop high‑potentials.
- **T‑3 years** – Formal succession plan, assess candidates.
- **T‑1 year** – Select successor, create transition plan.
- **Transition** – Knowledge transfer, stakeholder hand‑off, gradual handover.

### CEO / CTO Learning Agenda
- Strategic thinking
- Financial acumen
- Leadership presence
- Communication skills
- Decision‑making frameworks
- Technical depth (for CTO) – architecture, security, emerging tech
- Executive coaching, peer networks (YPO, EO, CTO Craft)

## Success Indicators
| Category | Indicators |
|---|---|
| **Strategic** | Vision clarity, strategy execution on track, market position improving |
| **Financial** | Revenue growth ≥ target, EBITDA margin improvement, cash runway ≥ 12 months |
| **Technical** | System uptime > 99.9 %, deployments > 1 /day, technical debt < 10 % |
| **Team** | Engagement > 8/10, attrition < 10 %, diversity metrics improving |
| **Stakeholder** | Board confidence high, investor NPS strong, customer NPS > 70 |

---

## Red Flags
- Missed strategic or financial targets for two consecutive quarters.
- High executive or engineering turnover (> 15 %).
- Strained board or investor relationships.
- Declining market share or increasing cash burn.
- Technical debt growth > 15 % capacity.
- Repeated security incidents or outages.
- Culture surveys showing low morale.
- Personal burnout signs in leadership.

---

## Checklist for Executive Decision Review
- [ ] Problem statement clearly defined.
- [ ] Relevant data and evidence collected.
- [ ] All viable options listed and evaluated.
- [ ] Stakeholder impacts analysed.
- [ ] Risks identified and mitigated.
- [ ] Implementation plan with milestones created.
- [ ] Success metrics defined.
- [ ] Communication plan prepared (internal & external).
- [ ] Board / investor briefing scheduled if needed.
