# Marketing Strategy & Demand Generation Playbook

**Purpose** – Provide a single, end‑to‑end framework for Series A+ SaaS startups that need to define their Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), create differentiated positioning, plan and execute product launches, run full‑funnel acquisition campaigns, and measure results across channels.

---

## When to Use
- Defining or validating ICP, buyer personas, and market segmentation.
- Building positioning, value‑proposition, and battle‑cards.
- Planning a product or feature launch (any tier).
- Designing a full‑funnel demand‑generation program (TOFU → MOFU → BOFU).
- Setting up paid‑media (LinkedIn, Google, Meta) or SEO programs.
- Establishing partnership or affiliate channels.
- Implementing multi‑touch attribution and reporting in HubSpot.

---

## 1. Strategic Foundation
### 1.1 ICP Definition (B2B SaaS)
| Dimension | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Firmographics** | 50‑5,000 employees; $5M‑$500M ARR; US, Canada, UK, DE, FR; Series A‑Growth stage |
| **Technographics** | Cloud‑first, API‑driven stack; integrates with Salesforce, Slack, etc. |
| **Psychographics** | Pain score ≥ 7/10; buying motive = efficiency, revenue growth; 2‑6 mo sales cycle |
| **Validation Checklist** | • ≥ 5 paying customers match profile<br>• Fastest sales cycles (≤ median)<br>• Highest LTV (≥ median)<br>• Churn < 5% annual<br>• Daily/weekly product usage<br>• NPS ≥ 9 willing to be reference | 
**HubSpot ICP Scoring** – Create a custom property `ICP Fit` (A‑perfect, B‑good, C‑ok, D‑poor). Score on firmographics, engagement, and product usage. Build a report: `Win rate by ICP Fit`.

### 1.2 Market Segmentation
- **By Company Size**: SMB (10‑200 emp, $100‑2k ACV), Mid‑Market (200‑2,000 emp, $2k‑50k ACV), Enterprise (2,000+ emp, $50k+ ACV).
- **By Vertical**: Choose 2‑3 focus verticals; start horizontal, add verticals as you scale.
- **By Geography**: US/CA (largest TAM), UK (gateway to EU), DACH (high‑value EU), France, Nordics.
- **Priority Matrix** – Rank each segment on TAM, sales‑cycle speed, win‑rate, and product fit. Allocate budget proportionally (e.g., US Mid‑Market = 50% of spend).

## 2. Positioning & Messaging
### 2.1 April Dunford Positioning Framework
1. **List True Alternatives** – direct competitors + status‑quo (spreadsheets, DIY, do‑nothing). 
2. **Identify Unique Attributes** – features, exclusive integrations, performance metrics.
3. **Map to Value** – translate each attribute into a concrete benefit and outcome (e.g., AI automation → save 10 hrs/week). 
4. **Best‑Fit Customers** – who cares most about the outcome.
5. **Category Choice** – compete head‑to‑head, own a niche, or create a new category.
6. **Trend Overlay** – remote work, AI adoption, data‑privacy regulations.

### 2.2 Messaging Architecture
**One‑liner (Value Proposition)**
```
[Product] helps [Target Customer] [Achieve Goal] by [Unique Approach]
```
**Hierarchy**
1. **Value Proposition** – single sentence.
2. **Key Benefits** – 3‑5 bullet points (speed, quality, cost, collaboration).
3. **Feature‑Benefit‑Outcome** rows.
4. **Proof Points** – logos, stats, case‑study snippets.

**Persona‑Specific Hooks**
| Persona | Core Concern | Message Tone | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic Buyer (VP) | ROI, revenue | Data‑driven, formal | “Increase revenue 25% while cutting tool spend $200k/yr.” |
| Technical Buyer (Engineer) | Architecture, security | Technical, objective | “99.99% uptime, SOC 2 compliant, native API.” |
| End User (Manager) | Ease of use | Friendly, practical | “Spend less time on busywork, more on impact.” |

### 2.3 Testing & Iteration
- **Qualitative** – 10‑15 customer interviews; ask them to describe the product in their own words.
- **Quantitative** – A/B test headlines, ad copy, and landing‑page variants for 2‑4 weeks.
- **Sales Feedback** – Monthly win/loss debrief; capture top objections and winning messages.
- **Iteration Cycle** – Test 2‑4 weeks → Analyze 1 week → Update docs 1 week → Train sales 1 week. Repeat quarterly.

## 3. Competitive Intelligence
### 3.1 Analysis Framework
| Tier | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1 – Direct** | Same category, similar ACV |
| **Tier 2 – Indirect** | Adjacent solutions, broader platforms |
| **Tier 3 – Status‑Quo** | Spreadsheets, in‑house builds, do‑nothing |

**Sources** – competitor trials, website monitoring, sales‑call recordings (Gong/Chorus), review sites (G2, Capterra), job postings, financial filings, social media, partner channels, industry reports.

### 3.2 Battlecard Template (per competitor)
```
COMPETITOR: [Name]
OVERVIEW: Founded, Funding, HQ, Employees, Customers, Pricing
POSITIONING: Their claim vs. reality
STRENGTHS: 1‑3 bullet points
WEAKNESSES: 1‑4 bullet points
OUR ADVANTAGES: 1‑4 bullet points
WHEN TO WIN: Conditions favoring us
WHEN TO LOSE: Conditions favoring them
TALK TRACKS: Objection handling scripts
PROOF POINTS: Case study, rating, win‑rate
```
- Store in Notion/Confluence.
- Update monthly or on major competitor releases.

### 3.3 Win/Loss Process
1. Identify closed‑won/lost deals (last 30 days).
2. Send interview request via HubSpot workflow.
3. Conduct 30‑45 min interview; record with permission.
4. Log themes in a spreadsheet: Reason, Competitor, Price, Gap, Messaging.
5. Produce a monthly insights report with action items for Product, Sales, and Marketing.

## 4. Go‑to‑Market (GTM) & Launch Playbooks
### 4.1 Motion Selection (Series A)
- **Hybrid PLG + Sales‑Led** – free trial for SMB, outbound demos for Enterprise.
- Benefits: Faster learning, broader TAM, efficient scaling.

### 4.2 90‑Day Launch Timeline (Tier 1 – Major Launch)
| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| -8 to -6 | Define ICP, positioning, battlecards; set launch KPIs |
| -5 to -4 | Build GTM plan, sales deck, demo script, content assets |
| -3 to -2 | Build landing pages, HubSpot campaign, press release, demo video |
| -1 | Final review, pre‑launch email to VIPs, press embargo lift |
| Day 1 | Press release live, email blast, social push, outbound blitz |
| Week 1‑2 | Monitor conversion, A/B test landing pages, sales SLA ≤ 4 h |
| Week 3‑4 | Collect early feedback, adjust messaging, publish case study |
| Week 5‑8 | Optimize under‑performing channels, scale winners |
| Week 9‑12 | Enter new segment or geography, expand partnership outreach |

### 4.3 International Market Entry Checklist (per market)
- Translate website, UI, and email (local language).
- Local currency pricing, VAT/Tax display.
- GDPR/CCPA compliance (double‑opt‑in, consent tracking).
- Local phone number & support hours.
- Hire or partner with local sales reps.
- Localized paid‑media mix (e.g., LinkedIn dominant in EU).
- Local case studies and testimonials.

## 5. Demand Generation Framework
### 5.1 Full‑Funnel Tactics
| Funnel Stage | Primary Tactics |
|---|---|
| **TOFU – Awareness** | Paid social (LinkedIn, Meta), display, content syndication, SEO (informational), co‑webinars |
| **MOFU – Consideration** | Paid search (solution keywords), retargeting, gated eBooks, email nurture, comparison pages |
| **BOFU – Decision** | Brand & competitor search, direct outreach, free‑trial CTAs, case studies, ROI calculators |

### 5.2 Campaign Brief Template (HubSpot)
```
Campaign Name: [Q2‑2025‑LinkedIn‑ABM‑Enterprise]
Objective: Generate 50 SQLs ($50k+ ACV) 
Budget: $15k/mo 
Duration: 90 days 
Channels: LinkedIn Ads, Retargeting, Email 
Audience: Directors+ at SaaS firms, 500‑5,000 emp, US/EU 
Offer: Gated Industry Benchmark Report 
Success Metrics:
  - Primary: 50 SQLs, <$300 CPO
  - Secondary: 500 MQLs, 10% MQL→SQL, 40% email open
HubSpot Setup:
  - Campaign ID: create in HubSpot
  - Lead scoring: +20 download, +30 demo request
  - Attribution: First‑touch + Multi‑touch
Handoff:
  - SQL criteria: Title + Company size + Budget confirmed
  - Routing: Enterprise SDR workflow
  - SLA: 4‑hour response
```

### 5.3 HubSpot Tracking Steps
1. **Create Campaign** – Marketing → Campaigns → New.
2. **Tag Assets** – Add campaign ID to all landing pages, emails, ads.
3. **UTM Structure**
```
utm_source={channel}&utm_medium={type}&utm_campaign={campaign-id}&utm_content={variant}&utm_term={keyword}
```
4. **Lead Scoring** – Settings → Marketing → Lead Scoring; add points for campaign actions.
5. **Attribution Report** – Use HubSpot’s multi‑touch (W‑shaped) model for hybrid motion.

## 6. Paid‑Media Playbooks
### 6.1 Channel Strategy Matrix
| Channel | Best For | CAC Benchmark | Typical Conv. Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn Ads | B2B, Enterprise, ABM | $150‑$400 | 0.5‑2% |
| Google Search | High‑intent BOFU | $80‑$250 | 2‑5% |
| Google Display | Retargeting, awareness | $50‑$150 | 0.3‑1% |
| Meta (FB/IG) | SMB, visual products | $60‑$200 | 1‑3% |
| YouTube | Demo videos, brand | $100‑$300 | 0.5‑1.5% |

### 6.2 LinkedIn Ads Structure
```
Account
└─ Campaign Group: [Enterprise‑ABM]
   ├─ Campaign 1: Awareness (Thought Leadership)
   │   └─ Ad Set: CTO/VP, US, Tech
   ├─ Campaign 2: Consideration (Lead‑Gen Forms)
   │   └─ Ad Set: Retargeted visitors
   └─ Campaign 3: Conversion (Demo CTA)
       └─ Ad Set: Website visitors
```
- **Targeting** – 50‑5,000 emp, Director+, SaaS/Tech industries, matched audiences (website retarget, email list).
- **Budget** – Start $50 day per campaign; increase 20% weekly if CAC < target.
- **Creative** – Thought‑leadership, social proof, problem‑solution, demo‑first.
- **Lead‑Gen Forms** – Higher conversion, sync to HubSpot; use landing pages for BOFU.

### 6.3 Google Search Playbook
- **Campaign Types** – Brand, Competitor, Solution, Product‑Category, Retargeting.
- **Ad Group Example** – `project management software`
  - Keywords: exact, phrase, BMM (e.g., `+project +management +software`).
  - Ads: 3 responsive search ads with 15 headlines, 4 descriptions.
- **Bid Strategy** – Manual CPC until 50 conversions → Target CPA → Max Conversions with tCPA.
- **Negative Keywords** – Maintain a list of 100+ (free, cheap, jobs, reviews).

### 6.4 Meta Ads Playbook (SMB)
- **Objective** – Conversions.
- **Ad Sets** – Lookalike 1% (purchasers), Interest (business tools), Retargeting (30‑day visitors).
- **Creative** – 15‑second video, first 3 seconds hook, captions, product UI demo.
- **Scaling** – If CAC < target for 2 weeks, increase spend 20%; pause if conversion rate drops >20%.

## 7. SEO Strategy
### 7.1 Technical Foundations (Quarterly Audit)
- XML sitemap submitted, robots.txt allow crawling.
- HTTPS, PageSpeed > 90 (mobile), Core Web Vitals passing.
- Structured data (Organization, Product, FAQ).
- Canonical tags, hreflang for each locale.
- Use Screaming Frog to find 404s, redirect chains, duplicate content, missing meta.

### 7.2 Keyword Tiers
| Tier | Intent | Example Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 – BOFU** | Commercial | `best project management software`, `[competitor] alternative` |
| **Tier 2 – MOFU** | Solution‑aware | `how to manage remote teams`, `project management tools for SaaS` |
| **Tier 3 – TOFU** | Informational | `what is project management`, `team collaboration challenges` |
- Prioritize Tier 1 for quick wins, then Tier 2, then Tier 3.
- For each market, translate and localize keywords (don’t just copy English terms).

### 7.3 On‑Page SEO Template
```
URL: /best-project-management-software
Title (≤60): Best Project Management Software 2025 | YourBrand
Meta Description (≤155): Compare top 10 tools, pricing, reviews. Find the perfect fit. Free trials available.
H1: Best Project Management Software in 2025
H2s: What is PM software? | Top 10 Tools Compared | Key Features | Pricing | How to Choose | FAQ
Content: 2,000‑3,000 words, natural 1‑2% keyword density, 3‑5 internal links, 2‑3 external authoritative links, 3‑5 images with alt text.
Schema: Product, FAQ, HowTo.
CTA: Above‑fold – Start Free Trial; Mid‑content – Compare Plans; End – Book Demo.
```
- Update Tier 1 pages quarterly, Tier 2 semi‑annually, Tier 3 annually.

### 7.4 Link‑Building Priorities
1. **Digital PR** – Publish original research, pitch journalists (HARO, Terkel).
2. **Guest Posts** – Target DA ≥ 40, avoid link farms.
3. **Co‑Marketing** – Partner content with complementary SaaS tools.
4. **Community** – Answer on Reddit/Quora, create calculators.
5. **Broken‑Link Replacement** – Offer your content for dead links on competitor sites.
- **Velocity** – 5‑10 new links/month for new domains; up to 20‑30/month after 6 months.

## 8. Partnerships & Affiliate Programs
### 8.1 Partner Tiering
| Tier | Type | Effort | Typical ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategic** | Co‑marketing, integration | 6‑12 mo to launch | 100+ leads/mo |
| **Affiliate** | Bloggers, reviewers | Medium (setup once) | 30‑50% CAC reduction |
| **Referral** | Existing customers | Low (automation) | 5‑10% of customers refer |
| **Marketplace** | App stores (HubSpot, Salesforce) | Medium | Brand visibility, low‑mid pipeline |

### 8.2 Partner Outreach Template
```
Subject: [YourBrand] ↔ [TheirBrand] Partnership Idea

Hi [Name],

I’m [Your Name] at [YourBrand]. We help [ICP] achieve [value prop].
I see a strong overlap with [TheirBrand]’s audience. Would you consider:
- Bi‑directional integration
- Joint webinar or case study
- Revenue‑share model

Let me know if you’d like to discuss.

Best,
[Your Name]
```
- Track outreach in HubSpot (Deal pipeline “Partner Outreach”).
- Define success metrics: leads, pipeline, revenue.

### 8.3 Affiliate Setup (Series A typical)
- Platform: **PartnerStack** (B2B SaaS) or **Rewardful** (Stripe integration).
- **Commission** – 30% recurring for 12 months (or $500 per SQL). Bonus tier for >10 referrals/quarter.
- **Recruitment** – Outbound to industry bloggers, inbound “Become an Affiliate” page, conference networking.
- **Enablement Kit** – Logos, product screenshots, pre‑written blog/social copy, unique UTM links.
- **Tracking** – HubSpot property `Partner Source`; UTM `utm_source=partner-name` captured automatically.

## 9. Attribution & Reporting (HubSpot‑Centric)
### 9.1 Model Selection
- **First‑Touch** – Shows discovery drivers (use for brand‑awareness reporting).
- **Last‑Touch** – Shows closing drivers (use for BOFU spend analysis).
- **Multi‑Touch (W‑Shaped)** – 40 % first, 20 % middle, 40 % last. Recommended for hybrid PLG/Sales‑Led.
- Set default to **W‑Shaped**; run side‑by‑side reports quarterly to validate.

### 9.2 Weekly Dashboard (HubSpot)
- **Traffic** – Visits, unique, bounce.
- **Leads** – MQLs, SQLs, conversion rates.
- **Pipeline** – Opportunities created, total value, velocity.
- **CAC** – Spend ÷ new customers.
- **Channel Mix** – Leads % by source, ROI per channel.
- **Dimensions** – By region, campaign, funnel stage.
- Review daily; optimize on the weekly meeting.

### 9.3 Monthly Executive Dashboard
| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Marketing‑Sourced Pipeline | $X M (≥ 30 % of total) |
| Marketing‑Sourced Revenue | $Y k (≥ 20 % of ARR) |
| Blended CAC | ≤ $Z (Series A benchmark) |
| MQL→SQL Rate | ≥ 15 % |
| Pipeline Velocity | ≤ 45 days |
| ROMI | ≥ 3:1 |
- Include top‑performing campaigns, under‑performing channels, next‑quarter experiment list, and budget reallocation recommendation.

## 10. Checklist
- **ICP & Segmentation** – Defined, validated, HubSpot scoring set.
- **Positioning** – Completed Dunford steps, one‑liner approved.
- **Battlecards** – Created for top 3 competitors, stored and shared.
- **Launch Plan** – 90‑day timeline, assets ready, sales enablement trained.
- **Demand Campaign** – Brief completed, HubSpot campaign created, UTM schema applied.
- **Paid Media** – LinkedIn, Google, Meta structures built; budgets allocated.
- **SEO** – Technical audit done, Tier 1 pages optimized, link‑building calendar set.
- **Partnerships** – Tier 1 partner identified, outreach sent, tracking property added.
- **Attribution** – Multi‑touch model active, weekly dashboard live.
- **Reporting Cadence** – Weekly performance review, monthly executive deck.
