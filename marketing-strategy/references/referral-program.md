# Referral & Affiliate Programs

Design and run programs that turn existing customers, partners, or influencers into acquisition channels.

---

## When to use
- You need a low‑cost acquisition channel that leverages word‑of‑mouth.
- Your product is shareable, has network effects, or benefits from social proof.
- You want to reach new audiences through affiliates or influencers.
- You have a budget for rewards and can allocate engineering or platform resources.

---

## Program Types
| Type | Ideal for | Typical rewards | Management effort |
|------|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Customer Referral** | B2C, low‑ticket SaaS, physical goods | Credit, free months, swag | Low |
| **Affiliate** | B2B, high‑ticket, content creators | Commission (% or flat) | Medium‑High |
| **Hybrid** | Companies that want both customer referrals and partner‑driven sales | Mix of credits and commissions | High |

---

## Referral Program Design Workflow
1. **Identify Trigger Moments** – ask users to refer after a high‑value event (first “aha” moment, milestone, upgrade, excellent support).  
2. **Choose Share Mechanism** – prioritize in‑product sharing, then personalized link, email invite, social share, and finally referral code.  
3. **Define Incentive Structure**  
   - *Single‑sided* (referrer only) – simple, works for high‑value products.  
   - *Double‑sided* (referrer + referred) – higher conversion, default for most programs.  
   - *Tiered* – adds gamification; use if you want ongoing engagement.  
4. **Size Incentives** – calculate a maximum reward:
   ```
   MaxReward = (LTV × GrossMargin) – TargetCAC
   ```
   Keep rewards within 10‑25 % of the first purchase for B2C, or 1‑3 months free for B2B SaaS.
5. **Build Tracking** – generate unique links or codes, store clicks, sign‑ups, and reward status.
6. **Launch & Promote** – announce via email, in‑app banner, and support channels.
7. **Measure & Iterate** – monitor key metrics, run A/B tests, and adjust incentives or prompts.

---

## Incentive Types & When to Use
| Incentive | Pros | Cons | Best for |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| Cash / credit | Universally valued | Transactional feel | Marketplaces, fintech |
| Product credit | Drives usage | Only useful if user will use it | SaaS subscriptions |
| Free months | Clear value | May attract freebie seekers | Subscription services |
| Feature unlock | Low cost | Limited appeal | Freemium models |
| Swag / gifts | Memorable, shareable | Logistics overhead | Brand‑focused companies |
| Charity donation | Aligns with mission | Lower personal motivation | Mission‑driven brands |

## Affiliate Program Design Highlights
- **Commission models**: 10‑30 % of first sale, flat fee per qualified lead, or 10‑25 % recurring revenue for subscriptions.
- **Cookie duration**: 24 h (impulse), 7‑14 d (e‑commerce), 30 d (SaaS), 60‑90 d (enterprise), lifetime for strategic partners.
- **Recruitment sources**: existing power users, niche bloggers, YouTubers, newsletters, complementary tool vendors, consultants.
- **Enablement assets**: tracking links, product briefs, audience profiles, creative banners, sample copy, demo access, FAQ, payment terms.

---

## Core Metrics
- **Viral coefficient (K‑factor)**: `K = invitations × conversionRate`. Aim for **K > 1** for self‑sustaining growth.
- **Referral rate**: `(referring customers) / (total customers)`. Good: 10‑25 %; great: >25 %.
- **Referrals per referrer**: average successful invites per active referrer. Target 2‑5.
- **Referral CAC**: `(total rewards + tool cost + mgmt time) / (number of referred customers)`.
- **Referral ROI**: `(revenue from referrals – program cost) / program cost`.
- **LTV uplift**: referred customers typically have 15‑25 % higher LTV and lower churn.

---

## Optimization Checklist
- **Increase referral rate**: improve prompt timing, reduce friction, test multiple reward types, add reminders.
- **Boost conversion**: optimize landing page for referred users, strengthen new‑user incentive, showcase referrer endorsement.
- **A/B tests**:
  - Reward amount (e.g., +10 %).
  - Reward type (cash vs. credit).
  - Single vs. double‑sided.
  - Prompt placement (in‑app banner vs. email).
  - CTA copy and email subject lines.
- **Fraud prevention**: email verification, device fingerprinting, IP monitoring, delayed payouts, minimum activity thresholds, clear TOS, caps per period, clawback on refunds.

## Email Sequences
### Launch Announcement
```
Subject: Earn [Reward] for sharing [Product]

We’ve launched a referral program! Share your unique link below. When a friend signs up, you both receive [Reward].

Your link: [URL]

[CTA: Share now]
```
### Nurture (non‑referrers)
- Day 7: “Did you know you can earn [Reward] by inviting friends?”
- Day 30: “Know anyone who’d love [Product]? Here’s your link.”
- Day 60: Success story + reminder.
### Re‑engagement for past referrers
```
Subject: Your friends love [Product]

[Friend] just achieved [milestone]. Want to help others? Earn [Reward] for each new sign‑up.

[Link]
```

## Launch Checklist
### Pre‑Launch
- [ ] Define goals & success metrics.
- [ ] Design incentive structure and calculate max reward.
- [ ] Select and configure a referral/affiliate platform.
- [ ] Build referral landing page and in‑app prompt.
- [ ] Draft email templates and support scripts.
- [ ] Set up tracking, attribution, and fraud rules.
- [ ] Write terms & conditions.
- [ ] Test end‑to‑end flow (invite → sign‑up → reward).
### Launch Day
- [ ] Send announcement email to existing customers.
- [ ] Activate in‑app referral widget.
- [ ] Publish program details on website.
- [ ] Notify support and sales teams.
- [ ] Monitor for fraud or technical issues.
### First 30 Days
- [ ] Review funnel metrics (invites, conversions, reward payouts).
- [ ] Identify top referrers and send thank‑you rewards.
- [ ] Collect user feedback on friction points.
- [ ] Implement first round of optimizations (e.g., prompt timing, reward tweaks).
- [ ] Send reminder emails to non‑referrers.

## Questions to Clarify Your Situation
1. Is the program a pure customer referral, an affiliate partnership, or a hybrid?  
2. What is your average customer LTV and current CAC?  
3. Do you have an existing program or are you starting from scratch?  
4. Which tools or platforms are you already using (CRM, payment gateway, analytics)?  
5. What is your budget for rewards, payouts, and platform fees?  
6. Does your product have natural sharing moments or network effects?
